DocDB is a fork. Releases made **before** Hanzo took stewardship on 2026-03-03
are the upstream project's work and their notes live with the project that wrote
them: <https://github.com/FerretDB/FerretDB/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md>. Restating
them here would put another project's release history under our brand and leave
two copies to drift; this file starts where our stewardship does.

are left out deliberately rather than listed as product releases they are not.
